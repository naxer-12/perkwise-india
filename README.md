# PerkWise India (भारत PerkWise) 🇮🇳

### The Unified Real-Time Knowledge Base for Personal Finance, Credit & Debit Perks, Hotel Loyalty, and Daily Life Operations

**PerkWise India** is an independent, mathematically rigorous, consumer-advocacy web application and statutory knowledge base. Built with a Linear-inspired, high-whitespace minimalist interface, it equips Indian consumers with actionable frameworks to capture maximum cost benefits on everyday transactions, eliminate hidden fees, leverage high-ROI memberships, and streamline daily life operations.

## 🌟 Key Architecture & Recent Overhaul

### 1. ⚡ Real-Time Data Provenance & Daily Autonomous Web Crawler Engine
To eliminate ambiguity about data freshness and bias, PerkWise India operates a dedicated **Data Provenance & Real-Time Sync Registry**:
- **Dual Cadence Architecture:**
  1. **Daily Autonomous Web Crawler Agent:** Runs daily scheduled crawls (2:00 AM IST) scanning gazettes, circulars, and bank tariff sheets, filtering potential candidates against PerkWise's core consumer-first mission. Includes 10 strategic verified consumer insights and transparent candidate evaluation logs.
  2. **Real-Time Statutory Gazette Engine:** Continuously tracks statutory regulatory changes across 36 official authorities (.gov.in, rbi.org.in, sebi.gov.in, etc.) with instant live re-verification.
- **Why It Is Real-Time:**
  1. **Statutory Regulatory Tracking:** Continuous monitoring against official gazettes and master circulars:
     - **Reserve Bank of India (RBI):** Master Direction on Credit & Debit Card Issuance (`RBI/2022-23/92`)
     - **National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI):** RuPay Credit Card on UPI Interchange & MDR Circulars
     - **Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA):** Civil Aviation Requirements on Passenger Rights & Delay Compensation (`CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV`)
     - **Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT):** Statutory limits under Sections `80CCD(1B)`, `80D`, and `115BAC`
  2. **Direct Bank MITC (Most Important Terms & Conditions):** All reward rates, lounge access criteria, and fee waiver thresholds are mapped to official bank tariff schedules (HDFC, SBI Card, Axis, ICICI, BOB, AU Small Finance, Federal Bank), with zero third-party affiliate bias.
  3. **Interactive Re-Verification Heartbeat:** Users can trigger the live `⚡ Re-Verify Sources` control, which audits all 25 statutory sources, verifies gazette checksums, and updates the real-time timestamp.
- **Direct Statutory Links:** Every card and article displays its exact regulatory reference code and a direct link to the official `.gov.in`, `rbi.org.in`, or bank root domain.

### 2. 📋 Structured "Fact Sheet" Standard (Zero Fluff, High Legibility)
All guides and cards have been overhauled into structured, fluff-free fact sheets:
- **1-Sentence Verdict:** Immediate, plain-English value summary.
- **Core Data Table:** Side-by-side specs (Joining/Annual Fee, Waiver Spend, Accelerated Rewards, Base Rewards, Monthly Caps, Lounge Access Criteria, Forex Markup).
- **Hidden Catches & Excluded Spends:** Unambiguous warning callouts detailing excluded categories (rent, utilities, wallet loads, school fees, fuel).
- **3-Step Immediate Action Plan:** Step 1 (Eligibility check), Step 2 (Official portal submission), and Step 3 (Day-1 security & auto-debit setup).
- **Statutory Source Citation:** Reference circular number, authority, and verification status.

### 3. 💬 Authentic Customer Reviews & "New" Badge Fallback
- **Verified Cardholder Reviews:** Authentic feedback from verified Indian cardholders across major metros (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai) detailing holding duration, real-world reward crediting, and customer service.
- **Clean `"New"` Badge Fallback:** Any card or scheme without verified customer reviews renders a prominent, clean `"New"` badge pill (*"New Card / Scheme — No Verified Reviews Yet"*) instead of an empty box.
- **Interactive Feedback Form:** Users who hold the card can submit their rating (1–5 stars), holding duration, and candid comments, which are persisted to `localStorage` and immediately update the aggregate rating.
